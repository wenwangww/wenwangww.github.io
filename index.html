Environmental & Urban Economics

Wen Wang

Assistant Professor
Division of Social Science
The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

I am an economist studying the impacts of environmental and urban policies on human welfare, household decisions, and market outcomes. My research draws on causal inference and structural methods to examine environmental regulation, housing markets, health, and spatial inequality.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Duke University in 2020.
